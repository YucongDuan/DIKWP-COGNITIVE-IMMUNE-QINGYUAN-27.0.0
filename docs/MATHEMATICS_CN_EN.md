# QINGYUAN Mathematics / 清源数学模型

For content or an interface event \(x\), QINGYUAN keeps a non-compensatory vector:

\[
Q(x)=(E,C,M,A,I,V,R,K,Q_c,H)
\]

where evidence integrity \(E\), context completeness \(C\) and corrigibility \(Q_c\) are protective dimensions; manipulation \(M\), addiction \(A\), interest opacity \(I\), vulnerability exploitation \(V\), relational externality \(R\), pseudo-knowledge risk \(K\), and observed harm \(H\) are risk dimensions.

No single scalar is authorised to label a person. Interventions are selected by hard gates and dimension-specific thresholds:

\[
Admissible(a)=Scope(a)\land Reversible(a)\land Appeal(a)\land NoViewpointPenalty(a)\land NoHumanWorthRanking(a)
\]

QINGYUAN maintains plural hypotheses rather than inferring a single hidden intent:

- truthful but uncomfortable;
- sincere but incomplete;
- commercial persuasion;
- manipulative or addictive mechanism;
- distress or help signal;
- satire, fiction or quotation where relevant.

An intervention is escalated only by observable mechanism and evidence, not by emotional tone alone.
