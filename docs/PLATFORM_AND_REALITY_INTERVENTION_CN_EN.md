# Active Intervention / 主动干预

## Digital-space actions

- preserve protected critical or distress expression;
- add evidence and uncertainty labels;
- restore omitted parties, conditions and exceptions;
- introduce share and purchase friction;
- disclose sponsorship, affiliate and upsell interests;
- pause autoplay and infinite scroll by user choice;
- locally quarantine high-risk pseudo-knowledge offers;
- reduce recommendation amplification pending a reviewable process;
- notify exposed users when a verified correction exists;
- record an outcome and repair receipt.

## Real-life actions

- create a burden and beneficiary map;
- use a 24-hour cooling-off period for high-pressure purchases or family ultimatums;
- test a reversible arrangement with a review date;
- separate violence or coercion cases from ordinary compromise;
- request professional, legal, medical or community support where appropriate;
- document actual outcomes, corrections and restitution.

Automatic external deletion, sanctions, payment or public accusation are outside the Alpha authority model.
