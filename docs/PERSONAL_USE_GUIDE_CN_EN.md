# Personal Use Guide / 个人使用指南

1. Paste or type the content into Content Clinic.
2. State whether it is news, criticism, sales, education, distress or another purpose.
3. Add interface cues such as autoplay, infinite scroll or repeated push notifications.
4. Review the ten-dimensional output, not only the primary class.
5. Open Context Restore and identify omitted parties and conditions.
6. For family content, use the Relationship Simulator and safety-first exception.
7. For paid knowledge, complete the seller, evidence, refund and upsell audit.
8. Use Share Gate before forwarding or purchasing.
9. Record the real outcome and any correction.
10. Export a local package and delete data you do not wish to retain.

Do not paste passwords, authentication codes, private keys, unredacted medical records, children’s identities or material that could expose a whistleblower.
