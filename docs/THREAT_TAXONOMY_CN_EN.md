# Threat Taxonomy / 威胁分类

QINGYUAN distinguishes content valence from harmful mechanism. A frightening but accurate safety warning may be socially valuable; cheerful misinformation may be harmful.

1. **False factual claims / 虚假事实** — claims contradicted by strong evidence.
2. **Unverifiable certainty / 难验证确定性** — high-confidence claims without a falsifier or source.
3. **Context stripping / 语境截断** — omission of affected parties, constraints, exceptions or burdens.
4. **Manipulation / 操纵** — shame, fear, identity pressure, urgency, conspiracy framing or moral coercion.
5. **Addictive architecture / 成瘾架构** — autoplay, infinite scroll, streaks, variable reward and outrage loops.
6. **Vulnerable-target exploitation / 脆弱群体利用** — targeting people under high stress, low information, debt, illness, grief or care burden.
7. **Pseudo-knowledge commerce / 伪知识商业化** — marketing funnels disguised as education, supported primarily by testimonials and fabricated certainty.
8. **Relational externality / 关系外部性** — zero-sum scripts that transfer burdens and turn partial truths into identity conflict.
9. **Coordinated amplification / 协同放大** — repeated distribution that manufactures apparent consensus.
10. **Verified outcome harm / 可验证结果伤害** — financial, medical, relational, safety or developmental harm observed after exposure.

Protected categories include criticism, whistleblowing, scientific dissent, grief, victim testimony, bad news and help-seeking. Protection does not immunise threats, harassment, fraud or fabricated evidence.
