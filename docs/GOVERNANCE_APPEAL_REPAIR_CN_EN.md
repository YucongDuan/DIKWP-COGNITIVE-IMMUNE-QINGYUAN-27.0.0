# Governance, Appeal and Repair / 治理、申诉与修复

Institutional use requires role separation among evidence collection, decision, appeal and benefit. A platform must not profit from declaring content harmful.

Minimum process:

1. identify the observable content or interface mechanism;
2. preserve the source and context;
3. state the evidence tier and uncertainty;
4. notify the affected publisher or user;
5. provide a correction and counterevidence route;
6. use reversible measures first;
7. separate protected expression from harmful conduct;
8. document who benefits and who bears the burden;
9. record real outcomes;
10. reverse, compensate and revise when a decision was wrong.

A verified correction should reach substantially the same audience as the harmful amplification where technically and legally possible. Any restitution proposal remains a draft until confirmed by competent parties and due process.
