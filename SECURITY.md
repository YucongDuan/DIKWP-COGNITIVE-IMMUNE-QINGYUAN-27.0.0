# Security Policy

QINGYUAN is an offline-first research prototype. Report security issues privately to the maintainer before public disclosure when a defect could expose personal data or enable coercive moderation.

## Prohibited deployment shortcuts

- Do not connect the Alpha client directly to compulsory disciplinary, policing, welfare, credit, employment, education, immigration or medical decisions.
- Do not use a QINGYUAN analysis as proof that a person is malicious, irrational or unfit.
- Do not silently capture browsing history, private messages, health records, identities or payment secrets.
- Do not enable automatic external deletion, punishment, payment, doxxing or account sanctions.
- Do not treat criticism, grief, whistleblowing, bad news or political disagreement as a harmful-content category.

Production deployment requires independent security review, data minimisation, access controls, appeal, rollback, logging and jurisdiction-specific legal review.
