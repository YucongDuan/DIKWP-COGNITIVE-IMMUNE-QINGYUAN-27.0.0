Mechanisms examined

  • False certainty and fabricated evidence
  • Context that omits affected people, conditions or burdens
  • Manipulation through shame, fear, identity conflict or urgency
  • Infinite scrolling, autoplay, streaks and variable rewards
  • Concealed sales funnels presented as knowledge
  • Verifiable harm and its continued amplification

Expression protected

  • Uncomfortable but accurate facts
  • Scientific counterexamples and criticism of authority
  • Whistleblowing, incident reports and survivor accounts
  • Grief, anxiety, distress and requests for help
  • Satire, literature and fiction with context retained
  • Corrections and appeals concerning this system

Key distinction

Emotional tone does not determine social value. An upsetting pollution report can serve the public interest; an encouraging or cheerful falsehood can cause real harm.

Five questions before sharing

  1. Is this a fact, inference, emotion, recommendation or advertisement?
  2. Which level of claim does the source support?
  3. Whose circumstances, conditions and costs are omitted?
  4. Who benefits from my sharing it, and who bears the risk?
  5. If it is wrong, can a correction reach the same audience?

Repair priorities

  1. Stop further amplification or continued selling
  2. Notify affected people and provide an understandable correction
  3. Restore withdrawal, refunds, deletion and appeal
  4. Repair direct harm and unfairly transferred burdens
  5. Revise triggers, commercial incentives and recommendation mechanisms
  6. Record actual results and revise the model

Expression that a negative-energy label must not suppress

  • Accurate bad news about pollution, accidents, illness and unemployment
  • Accounts of distress from affected people, caregivers and people under pressure
  • Criticism of governments, companies, platforms, family power and this system
  • Scientific counterexamples, failed experiments and unwelcome data
  • Grief, anxiety, anger and explicit requests for help

Protection does not excuse harmful conduct

Strong criticism remains protected. Fabricating evidence, threats, harassment, disclosure of private identities and real-world harm require assessment of the specific conduct rather than suppression of an uncomfortable viewpoint.

A requirement to express only happiness can itself suppress facts, obstruct help-seeking and protect powerful parties.

Minimum conditions for institutional use
  1. Separate sources from levels of claim
  2. Keep at least two structurally different explanations
  3. Begin with reversible interventions
  4. Notice, response, appeal, expiry and withdrawal
  5. Platforms must not directly profit from penalties
  6. Corrections should reach the original audience where possible
  7. Use observed harm, missed cases and false positives to revise the system
